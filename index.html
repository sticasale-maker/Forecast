Why we built VIZ FORECAST

Let's be honest: nothing beats a great day underwater—but only if the conditions are right. We got tired of the guesswork, so we built this tool to help our community determine if a site is "diveable" before packing the gear. Our data is powered by a proprietary wave transformation model, patiently tuned over years of real-world use by thousands of local divers, geekie details here.

This project is entirely non-profit and run by volunteers who love diving as much as you do.

A quick reminder: Always use your own judgment before entering the water. If the actual conditions differ from the forecast, please let us know via the Feedback form under More—that feedback is exactly how we continue to improve for everyone.

How to read the scores
Easy · 67–100%
Mostly settled, with little water movement at the entry.
Caution · 33–65%
Some surge or chop to be aware of. Pick your entry and exit with care.
Avoid · under 33%
Rough and exposed. Not a good time to dive this site.

What goes into the score. Every site starts at 100%. Swell, wind waves, wind chop and swell wrapping around headlands take points off, depending on which way the site faces. The tide can make those effects stronger or weaker, depending on the site.

Tap any site to see what is lowering its score and when it is best that day.
